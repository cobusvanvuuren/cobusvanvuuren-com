# Business Automation Assessment

Full offer page: https://cobusvanvuuren.com/audit/

## What it is

A structured diagnostic that maps every process in a business, ranks automation
opportunities by ROI, and delivers a costed 90-day implementation plan -- not generic AI
advice, and not a tool sold or resold.

## Process

1. **Intake (90 minutes)** -- a structured conversation covering workflows, team, and
   recurring bottlenecks. Done by voice call or written form.
2. **Analysis (5 business days)** -- every process assessed against an AI-readiness
   framework, automation opportunities ranked by ROI (highest-return first, no guessing).
3. **Delivery (written report + 30-minute call)** -- full written report with ranked
   findings and a prioritised 90-day implementation plan, walked through on a call.

## What's included

- A complete process map
- Ranked automation opportunities, ordered by ROI
- Specific tool and workflow recommendations scoped to the business's type, size, and
  existing stack
- A prioritised 90-day implementation plan
- A 30-minute delivery walkthrough call

## Pricing and guarantee

- R5,497 once-off.
- Guarantee: if the assessment doesn't identify at least R50,000 in annual savings
  opportunities specific to the business, full refund within 5 business days, no
  conditions.
- Implementation (if the client chooses to proceed) is a separate engagement,
  R20,000-R80,000, scoped after the assessment.

## Who it fits

SA professional services owner-operators: accountants, tax practitioners, attorneys,
financial advisors, GPs and specialists, consulting engineers, management consultants,
architects, property and letting agencies, trade and construction, multi-entity
operators. Any business where the owner's judgment is the bottleneck fits, regardless of
industry.

## Who it does not fit

- Businesses looking for an AI training course or workshop (this is not that)
- Businesses wanting an open-ended retainer (this is a bounded, once-off engagement)
- Anyone expecting off-the-shelf tool recommendations without process mapping first
