# SA Professional Services Automation Scoping

Context specific to automating South African accounting, legal, and financial advisory
practices, layered on top of the general triage method (see
/.well-known/agent-skills/automation-opportunity-triage/SKILL.md).

## Who this applies to

Owner-operators of SA professional services firms: accountants (CA/SAICA), attorneys,
financial advisors (CFP/FAIS). Typically 3-20 staff, R2m-R15m annual revenue. These
businesses bill by the hour, so manual admin is a direct revenue loss, not just an
inconvenience.

## Regulatory context that shapes what's automatable

- **POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act)**: client data handling, storage, and
  processing in any automated workflow must account for POPIA obligations -- this affects
  what can be sent to third-party tools and how client records are retained.
- **FICA (Financial Intelligence Centre Act)**: applies to client identity verification
  and record-keeping for financial advisors and certain legal work; automation of intake
  must not bypass FICA-required checks.
- **SARS eFiling**: much SA accounting admin is structured around SARS submission
  deadlines and formats -- automation opportunities often cluster around data prep for
  filing, not the filing itself.

## Common high-ROI candidates in this sector

- Client intake and document collection (currently manual chasing)
- Recurring compliance deadline tracking
- Draft preparation for recurring filings (with a human review step retained)
- Call and meeting note capture for firms where advice is given verbally then
  re-typed into records

## What this is not

Not legal or compliance advice. Any automation touching POPIA/FICA-regulated data or
process should be reviewed against current legislation and the firm's own compliance
obligations before implementation -- this skill scopes opportunity, not compliance
sign-off.
